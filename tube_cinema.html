Trust matters

A finance site built around transparency.

Net worth is rarely an exact public number. WealthScope should clearly distinguish reported facts from estimates, explain methodology and link to credible sources where appropriate.

Editorial note: Never present an estimate as a confirmed fact. Add a “Sources & methodology” section to every profile.
✓ Original research
Write useful, human-readable articles rather than copying other sites.
✓ Sources & methodology
Explain where reported figures come from and when they were updated.
✓ Clear disclosures
Use privacy, terms, contact and financial-information disclaimers.
✓ Reader-first ads
Keep ad placements visible but avoid misleading users or encouraging clicks.
